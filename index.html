Daily Mystery

Why does popcorn pop?

It's not magic — it's science! But how exactly does heat turn a tiny kernel into a fluffy snack?

Each popcorn kernel contains a tiny drop of water inside a coat of hard starch. As the kernel heats up, the water turns into steam, creating intense pressure. When the hull gives way... POP! The starch inflates into a fluffy foam.
